  A film program presented by Monokino in O.666, Ostend

  27 November at 20:00


  How do we relate to what disappears, remains hidden, or is only partly visible? The films in this programme do not approach the world as something that can be fully known. They search for other forms of nearness and knowing, through analogue film, memory, language, voices, gestures and attentive presence.


  Animals, spirits, humans and landscapes are not separate categories here. They are intertwined. The ocean inscribes itself onto the emulsion, voices carry memories onward, language keeps alive relationships with places, histories and non-human worlds.


  The films in this programme move between mourning and tribute. They pause over what was lost or threatens to drift away, and look for ways to stay connected to what seems absent. An invitation to listen to what remains: traces, stories, water, memory.


  Film program:

  What the Water Said (1-3) (David Gatten, 1997, 16’)

  See Through the Hollowed Blue Hellebore (Jiayi Chen, 2026, 18’)

  He Who Wears Faces on His Ears (Sky Hopinka, 2025, 9’)

  El mar peinó a la orilla (Valentina Alvarado Matos, 2019, 5’)
